# Lesson Specification: Greetings & Manners

> **Status**: `APPROVED & IMPLEMENTED`  
> **Lesson ID**: `les-u1-01`  
> **Unit**: `Unit 1: Korean Foundations`  
> **Target Level**: `Beginner (Level 1)`  
> **Estimated Duration**: `10 mins`  

---

## 1. Learning Objectives
- Master standard Korean greetings for meeting someone and taking leave.
- Understand the difference between polite formal (`존댓말`) and casual (`반말`) greetings.
- Practice proper bow etiquette during formal greetings.

---

---

## 3. Cultural & Honorific Notes

- **Bow Depth**: A 15° to 30° bow accompanies `안녕하세요` and `감사합니다` when greeting elders, teachers, or supervisors.
- **Staying vs. Leaving**:
  - `안녕히 가세요` literally means "Go in peace" (used when the other person is departing).
  - `안녕히 계세요` literally means "Stay in peace" (used when you are departing and the other person remains).
